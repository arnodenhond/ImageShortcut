BIG APP ICON HERE

Create shortcuts in your launcher to your favorite images.

Do you often need to see an important image but its a difficult to find in your image gallery? Create a shortcut and view the image immediately!

If you have many important images it will not take up a lot of home screen space. Since these are not widgets, they can be grouped together into folders.

# Frequently Asked Questions

*If I delete the image after creating a shortcut, what happens?*

> A local copy of the image is kept so the shortcut always keeps working.

*Will the local copy be kept forever?*

> When you remove the shortcut, the copy is also removed.

*What does it cost?*

> It's free! No subscriptions, no in app purchases and no advertising.
